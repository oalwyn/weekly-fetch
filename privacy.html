Privacy Policy

The Bay Area Weekend Fetch: the website and the “Bay Fetch” iPhone app · Effective November 11, 2026

The short version

  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Almost everything stays on your phone.
  • Only if you use shared plans do we store anything: a random list code, the first name you type, and the events you starred or locked in.
  • We never sell or share your information with anyone for advertising.

What stays on your device

These are saved only on your phone or browser and are never sent to us:

  • Your stars and locked-in plans (when you're not using shared plans), your filters and view settings.
  • Your “home” spot for distances. If you tap Use my current location, your location is read once, rounded, and used on your device to show how far away events are. It is never sent to us. If you type an address, city or ZIP instead, only that text is sent to OpenStreetMap's address lookup to find it on the map (see below).
  • A saved copy of the event list, so the app works without a signal.

Deleting the app (or clearing the website's data in your browser) removes all of this.

Shared plans (optional)

If you start or join a shared plan, the following is stored in our database so everyone on the plan sees the same list:

  • A list code: a random 24-character code created on your phone. It is not linked to your phone, email or Apple ID.
  • Your first name, exactly as you type it, so others can see who starred what. You can use a nickname.
  • The events you star or lock in, the day you locked them in, and a copy of the public event details (title, time, place, link).

Anyone who has your invite link can see and change that list, so share it only with people you plan with. The database is hosted by Supabase on our behalf.

To remove your information, unstar your events (this removes your name from them). When the last person unstars an event, it and its plans are deleted. To have an entire shared list deleted, contact us (below) with its list code.

iPhone features

  • Reminders: if you allow notifications, reminders for your locked-in plans are scheduled on your phone. Nothing about them is sent to us.
  • Add to Calendar: opens Apple's New Event screen, filled in. The app can't see or read your calendar. You choose whether to add the event.
  • Invites: the share sheet sends the invite link using whichever app you pick (Messages, Mail and so on). We don't see who you send it to.

Services we rely on

Like any website, these services receive standard technical information (such as your IP address and device type) when the app or website loads content from them:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) hosts the website and the event list the app downloads.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC) provides the fonts.
  • Supabase stores shared plans, as described above.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim (OpenStreetMap Foundation) turns a home address you type into a map position. It receives only what you type. See the OSM privacy policy.

Links to event pages and to Google Maps directions open those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app lists family events but is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/oalwyn/weekly-fetch/issues.
