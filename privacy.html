Privacy Policy

The Weekend Fetch: The Bay Area Weekend Fetch and Sydney Harbour Fetch websites, the “Weekend Fetch” iPhone app and the Android app · Updated November 16, 2026

The short version

  • No ads and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites. We never sell your information.
  • Browsing events needs no account, and most things stay on your phone.
  • If you sign in to plan weekends with a group, we store your email address (or Apple sign-in), the first name you choose, your groups, and the events you star or lock in.
  • We keep simple usage records (for example, that an event was starred or its ticket link was tapped) so we can see what's useful. We keep them for 13 months, never sell them and never use them for ads.
  • The website (not the iPhone or Android app) also uses Google Analytics to measure visits, with Google's advertising features turned off. It's skipped if your browser sends a “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control signal.
  • You can delete your account, and what's linked to it, from inside the app at any time.

What stays on your device

These are saved only on your phone or browser and are never sent to us:

  • Your stars and locked-in plans when you're not signed in to a group, plus your filters, view settings and the city you picked.
  • Your “home” spot for distances. If you tap Use my current location, your location is read once, rounded, and used on your device to show how far away events are. It is never sent to us. If you type an address, city or ZIP instead, only that text is sent to OpenStreetMap's address lookup to find it on the map (see below).
  • A saved copy of the event list, so the app works without a signal.

Deleting the app (or clearing the website's data in your browser) removes all of this.

Accounts and groups (optional)

You only need an account to plan with other people. When you sign in, we store:

  • Your email address, if you sign in with an emailed code. We use it only to send you sign-in codes, never for marketing.
  • Your Apple sign-in, if you use Sign in with Apple. Apple gives us an account identifier and an email address, which can be a private relay address if you choose “Hide My Email”. Apple may also share your first name the first time.
  • The first name you choose, shown to people in your groups next to the events you star. You can use a nickname and change it any time.
  • Your groups: each group's name, its members' first names, and its join code.
  • The events you star or lock in for a group, the day you locked them in, and a copy of the public event details (title, time, place, link).

People in your groups can see your first name and what you starred or locked in for that group. Anyone with a group's join code or invite link can join it, so share it only with people you plan with. A group's owner can make a new code at any time, which stops old invite links from working.

Usage records

To understand what's useful and improve the app, we record simple events:

  • opening the app or website, and switching to a new session after 30 minutes away;
  • opening an event in a plan or on the map, choosing a filter or distance, saving your interests (which kinds of events you hide), and a filtered list coming up empty;
  • starring or un-starring an event, locking it in to a day, moving or unlocking it, answering Going / Maybe / Can't, and setting or clearing a meet-up (we record that a meet-up was set, not the place you typed);
  • creating, joining or leaving a group, opening the share sheet for an invite and completing or copying it, viewing “All my plans”, and signing in;
  • adding a plan to your calendar (on iPhone, when iOS reports it was saved; Android doesn’t report this) and tapping a reminder;
  • tapping an event's “Tickets”, “More info”, “Directions” or movie “Showtimes” link (we record the website's name, such as ticketmaster.com, not the full address), and tapping the App Store link.

Each record has the kind of event, which listing it was about (with its category and area), the time, which city's edition you were using (such as the Bay Area or Sydney), whether it came from the website, the iPhone app or the Android app, the app version, a random session code that changes after 30 minutes away or when you sign in or out, and, for the first record of a session, how you arrived: campaign tags in the link (such as utm_source), an invite link, a search engine, or the name of the website that linked to us. We never record the rest of a link or address, what you type, your home location, invite codes, names or emails in these records. Changes to a group's plan are recorded by our server when they're saved. Each record also has one of:

  • if you're signed in, your account (and the group, for group activity);
  • if you're not, a random device code created on your device. It isn't your phone's advertising identifier and isn't connected to your name, email or account. Signing out replaces it with a new one.

We keep these records for 13 months. After that we keep only weekly totals that aren't connected to any account or device. We don't use them for advertising, and we don't combine them with information from other companies. We may share totals that can't identify anyone (for example, “120 people opened this event's page this month”) with event organizers or partners.

Older shared plans

Before accounts, shared plans used an anonymous list code, your first name as typed, and your starred events. Those plans keep working for now. When you sign in, you can move a plan into a group; its stars and locked-in days are copied over. Old shared plans will be retired in a future version.

Deleting your information

  • Delete your account in the app or on the website: Our Plans (called Our Weekend Plans in older app versions) → Group & account → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account, your name and your stars from every group. Groups you were in stay for their other members. Usage records you created stay as individual records but are no longer connected to you or your account.
  • Activity while signed out is connected only to a device code. To have it deleted, contact us (below) with that code. You'll find it at the bottom of Our Plans (Our Weekend Plans in older app versions) in the app and on the website.
  • Leave a group to remove your stars from it. When the last person leaves, the group is deleted.
  • For anything else, including deleting an older shared plan, contact us (below).

iPhone and Android app features

  • Reminders: if you allow notifications, reminders for your locked-in plans are scheduled on your phone. Nothing about them is sent to us, apart from the usage count when you tap one.
  • Add to Calendar: opens your phone's own new-event screen (Apple Calendar on iPhone, your calendar app on Android), filled in. The app can't see or read your calendar. You choose whether to add the event.
  • Invites: the share sheet sends the invite link using whichever app you pick (Messages, Mail, WhatsApp and so on). We don't see who you send it to.

Google Analytics (website only)

The website at theweekendfetch.com uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people visit, how they find us (for example, from a search) and which parts they use, such as switching tabs or tapping an event's “Tickets”, “More info” or “Showtimes” link. Google Analytics sets first-party cookies and receives your browser's IP address, device and browser type, approximate location (city level), the pages and events viewed, and the page that referred you. Google says it doesn't store IP addresses for Google Analytics 4.

  • We've turned off Google signals and ad personalization, so this information isn't used to show you ads or linked to your Google account by us.
  • We don't send your name, email, groups or plans to Google Analytics.
  • If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or “Do Not Track” signal, Google Analytics doesn't load at all.
  • You can also block it with Google's opt-out browser add-on, or by clearing and blocking cookies for this site.
  • The iPhone and Android apps don't use Google Analytics.

See how Google uses information from sites that use its services and the Google privacy policy.

Services we rely on

These companies process information for us only to run the app. Like any website, they also receive standard technical information (such as your IP address and device type) when the app or website contacts them:

  • Supabase runs our sign-in and database (accounts, groups, plans, usage counts). See the Supabase privacy policy.
  • Resend sends our sign-in code emails, so it receives your email address and the code. See the Resend privacy policy.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple if you use it, and distributes the iPhone app.
  • Google Play (Google LLC) distributes the Android app.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) hosts the website and the event list the app downloads.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC) provides the fonts.
  • Google Analytics (Google LLC) measures website visits, as described above.
  • Open-Meteo provides the weekend rain forecast on the Rainy Day tab. When you open that tab, your browser asks Open-Meteo for the forecast at your home location rounded to about 10 km (one decimal place), and keeps the answer on your device for three hours. Open-Meteo receives that rounded location and standard technical information, but nothing else about you. See the Open-Meteo privacy notes.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim (OpenStreetMap Foundation) turns a home address you type into a map position. It receives only what you type. See the OSM privacy policy.

Links to event pages and to Google Maps directions open those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Sydney Harbour Fetch and visitors in Australia

This policy covers Sydney Harbour Fetch (theweekendfetch.com/sydney/) in the same way as the Bay Area site: the same information, the same choices and the same services.

  • Where your information is kept: the services listed above are run by companies based outside Australia, so information you give us (such as your sign-in email, first name, groups and plans, and usage records) may be stored and processed overseas, including in the United States. We only use providers that process it for us to run the site, as described above.
  • Choosing a city is saved only in your browser. If your device's time zone suggests you're near another city's edition, we may suggest switching; this uses your time-zone setting on your device and nothing is sent to us.
  • Access, correction and complaints: you can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you, or make a privacy complaint, by contacting us (below). We'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Children

The app lists family events but is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
