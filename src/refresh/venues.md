# Venue and council checklist (read by every refresh run)

Every refresh must go through its city's list below, in addition to its own prompt: open each venue's or council's
own "what's on" page and add the events in the window that fit the site (and aren't already listed). Big ticketing sites
are only for discovering events; the item's `url` stays the venue's or organiser's own page (see the prompt's link rules).
If a venue's site has moved or the venue has closed or been renamed, note it in the final message rather than guessing.
Use your judgement on fit: skip private hire, corporate events, and anything already in the list.
