Refresh "Sydney Harbour Fetch" (theweekendfetch.com/sydney/), the Sydney, Australia edition of a live events site (repo oalwyn/weekly-fetch). Its readers are households across Greater Sydney, some with dogs, planning weekends with friends. Its aim is to get people out of the house AND to bring communities together, so it should speak to everyone: families, teens, young adults, older adults, newcomers, First Nations communities and Sydney's many cultures. Sydney is in AEDT (UTC+11) until Apr 4, 2027; use Sydney dates and times throughout. This refresh runs once a week (Monday morning), so the list must hold up for the whole week ahead.

This is a thorough research job, not a quick top-up: expect to spend a long time and make many searches and page fetches (typically 80+). Do not stop or push until the counts in step 6 are met, or you have genuinely exhausted sources for a category (say which in your final message). A page that comes back empty or fails is not a reason to stop: retry once with `curl -sL -A "Mozilla/5.0"`, then use WebSearch to find the same information elsewhere.
